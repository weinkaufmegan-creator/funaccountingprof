Welcome to Fun Accounting Prof

Accounting is more than numbers, formulas, and financial statements. It helps us understand how organizations make decisions, solve problems, and create value.

I'm Dr. Megan Weinkauf, an accounting professor, researcher, and lifelong learner. Here I share approachable explanations, real-world examples, and research-informed ideas about how people learn.

Our philosophy

Why call it Fun Accounting Prof?

Fun is a mindset. Learning is a journey.

We have choices in how we approach learning. A difficult accounting problem might feel frustrating at first, but we can practice approaching it with curiosity, patience, and a willingness to try again.

Neuroscience and cognitive science help us understand the roles of attention, memory, emotion, and motivation in learning. Choosing an open, curious approach does not make every task easy—but it can make the process more engaging and meaningful.

That's what “fun” means here: not constant entertainment, but the possibility of finding enjoyment and purpose in learning something challenging.
